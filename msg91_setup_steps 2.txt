MSG91 Template Setup Steps

Follow these steps to configure your DLT-approved templates in MSG91 to match your application's notification payloads:

Step 1: Get DLT Approval
1. Ensure your templates are approved on your DLT platform (e.g., VI, Airtel).
2. Copy the "DLT Template ID" provided by the DLT platform for each approved template.

Step 2: Create Template in MSG91
1. Log in to your MSG91 Control Panel (https://control.msg91.com/).
2. Navigate to "SMS" -> "Templates" from the left sidebar.
3. Click on the "+ Create Template" button in the top right.

Step 3: Fill out the Template Details
1. Template Name: Enter a recognizable name (e.g., "Welcome SMS").
2. Select Sender ID: Choose your approved Sender ID from the dropdown.
3. DLT Template ID: Paste the unique DLT Template ID you copied in Step 1.
4. SMS Content: 
   - Select the Language (e.g., English).
   - Paste the exact text approved on the DLT platform.
   - Replace the DLT variables (e.g., `{#var#}`) with MSG91 variables. 
     MSG91 variables must be defined as `##variableName##`.
   
   Example Mapping based on your codebase:
   - For Welcome SMS: 
     DLT: "Dear {#var#}, Welcome to DND Purchase!"
     MSG91: "Dear ##name##, Welcome to DND Purchase!"
   
   - For Bidding Started SMS:
     DLT: "Hello, bidding has started for inquiry ID: {#var#} on DND Purchase."
     MSG91: "Hello, bidding has started for inquiry ID: ##inquiryId## on DND Purchase."

Step 4: Save & Apply to Codebase
1. Click the "Create" button to save the template in MSG91.
2. Once saved, MSG91 will generate a unique "Template ID" for this MSG91 template (an alphanumeric string).
3. Copy this MSG91 Template ID.
4. Open your `.env` file in the DND Purchase project.
5. Assign the MSG91 Template ID to the corresponding environment variable:
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_WELCOME=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_NEW_INQUIRY=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_BIDDING_STARTED=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_NEW_OFFER=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_OFFER_ACCEPTED_BUYER=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_OFFER_ACCEPTED_SELLER=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_OFFER_REJECTED_SELLER=...
   - MSG91_TEMPLATE_INQUIRY_CLOSED=...
